ABOUT @OneDroid

OneDroid is an open-source initiative focused on building simple, secure, and privacy-friendly softwares.

GitHub LinkedIn
(01) WE VALUE

OneDroid is an open-source platform dedicated to building modern Android applications with a strong focus on transparency, privacy, and usability. We develop apps in the open so anyone can review the source code, understand how things work, and contribute to making them better. Openness and collaboration are fundamental to how we build software.

Privacy is another fundamental principle behind OneDroid. Our apps are designed to respect users by avoiding unnecessary tracking, intrusive ads, and hidden data collection. Instead, we focus on creating reliable tools that work exactly as intended while protecting users’ digital freedom.

OneDroid exists to deliver practical solutions that solve everyday problems without compromising trust or user experience. By keeping our projects open, free, and community-focused, we aim to build a sustainable ecosystem of Android apps that anyone can use, improve, and rely on.

(02) Maintainer

OneDroid is initiated and actively maintained by Tawhid Monowar, who leads the engineering efforts and long-term development strategy of the organization. He designs, builds, and maintains the core applications while ensuring the projects remain open source, secure, and aligned with the mission.
